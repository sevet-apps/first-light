# Narrator prompt for Google AI Studio (speech generation)

**Where:** Google AI Studio → *Generate speech* (Gemini TTS), **single-speaker** mode.
**Voices to try (female):** `Sulafat` (warm), `Despina` (smooth), `Gacrux` (mature), `Achernar` (soft). Make 2–3 takes per voice, keep the best.
**Settings:** temperature ≈ 1.0. Download as WAV.

Paste everything between the lines into the style / prompt field (if the UI has separate *style instructions* and *text* fields, put the "DIRECTOR'S NOTES" part into style and the "TRANSCRIPT" part into text).

---

AUDIO PROFILE: A woman in her thirties, the narrator of a cinematic opening film. Low, velvety, intimate voice with a soft breathy edge. Confident, unhurried, quietly seductive — she is not selling anything, she is inviting you into the adventure of your life. Native American-English accent, crisp consonants, warm vowels.

SCENE: Night. She speaks close to the microphone in a quiet, dark studio while the film plays: a rocket on the launch pad in silence, ignition, liftoff, the sky turning black, the Earth turning blue, a satellite opening its solar wings above the planet. Deep space ambience; she is calm and completely in control, savouring every word.

DIRECTOR'S NOTES:
- Pace: very slow and deliberate, about 1.5–2 words per second. Let every line breathe.
- Each item is a number, a short pause, then the phrase. Say the number softly and slightly lower, as if counting down a secret; then deliver the phrase with a subtle rise of wonder.
- Leave about one full second of silence after each item. Stretch the ellipses ("…") into real pauses.
- Never shout, never sound like an advertisement. Low energy on the surface, strong emotion underneath. A slight smile on the final line.
- The last line: a long pause after "Ten.", then "Welcome to First Light." — warm, intimate, the name "First Light" landing softly and with weight.
- Read ONLY the transcript. Do not read the labels or these notes. Do not add any words.

TRANSCRIPT:
One. The silence.
Two. The fire.
Three. A million parts… rising as one.
Four. Letting go.
Five. The sky turns black…
Six. …and the Earth turns blue.
Seven. Wings… open to the Sun.
Eight. A new eye on our world.
Nine. Built by your hands.
Ten. …… Welcome to First Light.

---

**Target length:** about 30–34 seconds. If a take is much faster, regenerate with "Pace: even slower, with two full seconds of silence between items" added to the notes.
**If the model reads the notes aloud:** put only the TRANSCRIPT in the text field and paste the AUDIO PROFILE / SCENE / DIRECTOR'S NOTES into the style field.
